# Go-live note for Kemi

**What it does.**
- Answers WhatsApp customers day and night, checks stock by size, and haggles only down to your
  minimum.
- Holds the item for 30 minutes and sends a Paystack link.
- Takes stock off only when Paystack confirms the full amount.
- Tells customers by itself when payment lands or fails, when their hold is ending, and when a
  sold-out size is back.
- Passes complaints, refunds and disputes to you.
- Your back-office assistant briefs you at 8pm and makes changes when you chat with it, using your
  PIN.

**What it doesn't do.** Take real money yet, give refunds (you approve each one), arrange
deliveries, or read photos and voice notes.

**Before real customers:**
1. **Payments.** Switch to your verified Paystack *live* key, do one real ₦100 purchase, and refund it.
2. **WhatsApp number.** Connect it. After 24 hours of silence from a customer, WhatsApp only allows
   pre-approved message templates, so set those up for reminders.
3. **Customer data (NDPA 2023).** Tell customers you store their number and orders. Delete them on
   request.
4. **Alerts.** Handovers go to your email (check it twice a day). Stock problems appear in your 8pm
   brief. Name a backup for when you're away.
5. **Stock.** Update it the same day you sell in the shop, or it will oversell.
6. **Cost.** About 0.1 Chord credit per customer message. Follow-ups are free. Set a low-balance
   alert.
